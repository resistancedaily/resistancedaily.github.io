  3. 2 Nov

    Two missing Kuki men found dead in Manipur

    Lives lost

    Manipur Police said on Sunday that two Kuki civilians missing from Taphou Kuki village since Wednesday were found dead, Scroll reports, taking deaths in Naga-Kuki clashes since February to 68. Naga groups led by the NPO question the recovery's timeline and seek an impartial inquiry; no police response to them was reported.

    Scroll · Northeast Now · Edition of Mon 2 Nov
  4. 1 Nov

    Three Kuki men killed in Noney gunfight

    Lives lost

    Three Kuki men were killed on Saturday in a gunfight between armed groups at Kekru Naga village, police said, adding that security had been stepped up. The Kuki Inpi Manipur said the dead were village volunteers and blamed Naga armed groups; police have not identified those responsible.

    The Hindu · Indian Express · Edition of Sun 1 Nov

  6. 25 Oct

    Imphal sit-in against any division of Manipur

    Assembly

    Five civil society groups led by AMUCO held a sit-in in Imphal East on Saturday, starting a statewide agitation against any administrative division of Manipur; their six demands include repeal of AFSPA and rehabilitation of displaced people. No police action or government response was reported.

    NE Now · Edition of Sun 25 Oct
  7. 24 Oct

    Tear gas at Imphal protest over killings

    Assembly

    Thousands joined a 'sound protest' in five valley districts on Friday against continuing killings, four days after three people were killed in Kangpokpi district. Police fired tear gas near the governor's residence in Imphal; no injuries were reported. The state Home Minister says three people have been held.

    Scroll · ThePrint / PTI · Edition of Sat 24 Oct
  8. 23 Oct

    Manipur valley sit-ins; students detained near Kangla

    Assembly

    Sit-ins were held across Manipur's valley districts on Thursday over the killing of three firewood collectors at Khongban Sunurok; schools stayed shut. Security forces clashed with student marchers near Kangla Gate in Imphal and several people were detained. No police or government statement on Thursday's protests was reported.

    NE Now · EastMojo · Edition of Fri 23 Oct
  9. 22 Oct

    Tear gas in Imphal; Kuki man shot dead

    Lives lost

    Police fired tear gas on Wednesday as protesters over Monday's killing of three Meitei firewood collectors tried to enter an Imphal event attended by the Chief Minister; three people had minor injuries. A Kuki man, Paolen Khongsai, was shot dead on Tuesday. Police have arrested three suspects in Monday's ambush.

    The Tribune · The Morning Voice · Edition of Thu 22 Oct
  10. 21 Oct

    Shutdown in Manipur valley after villagers killed

    Assembly

    Markets, schools and offices shut across five valley districts on Tuesday in a 48-hour strike after three Meitei villagers were shot dead on Monday; organisers demand arrests within 48 hours. Officials said extra forces were deployed, and police reported no incidents.

    National Herald · Imphal Times · Edition of Wed 21 Oct
  11. 20 Oct

    Four killed in two days in Manipur

    Lives lost

    Police say suspected militants shot dead three Meitei villagers collecting firewood near the Sunurok hills on Monday and injured three, with three more missing, a day after a man died in a clash between two communities in Thoubal district; the Chief Minister condemned the killings and security forces began a combing operation. Police fired tear gas when protesters placed bodies outside the Chief Minister's residence in Imphal.

    Times of India · New Indian Express · Edition of Tue 20 Oct
  12. 19 Oct

    28 homes torched in Kamjong in a day

    Lives lost

    Police say at least 28 houses of Naga and Kuki families were set on fire in Kamjong in 24 hours, after an attack that killed a Kuki civilian; residents fled to forests and an Army camp. Police deployed a joint force and the Deputy Chief Minister called the arson an intolerable crime; no one has been held responsible.

    PTI via Rediff · Edition of Mon 19 Oct
  13. 18 Oct

    One killed in attack on Manipur border village

    Lives lost

    Armed men attacked Chassad village on Saturday, killing one man and injuring four, including the village chief. Kuki organisations blame NSCN-IM cadres; no official finding on responsibility appeared in these reports.

    The Hindu · Economic Times · Imphal Times · Edition of Sun 18 Oct
  14. 17 Oct

    Kuki–Naga violence deepens in Manipur hills

    Lives lost

    ACLED data cited by NDTV counts nearly 100 deaths in 300+ incidents from 1 January to 10 October, including 19 in September. Kuki and Naga groups blame each other; accused groups deny involvement and no official inquiry has fixed responsibility.

    NDTV · Edition of Sat 17 Oct
