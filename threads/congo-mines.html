    Congo police detain over 500 at Kambove mine

    Detention

    Police in Haut-Katanga said more than 500 people were detained in a special operation at the Kambove mining site near Likasi, Actualité.cd reported on Sunday. The provincial police chief said it targeted people illegally occupying public-service roles at the site; no charges or access to lawyers were reported.
