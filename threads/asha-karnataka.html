ASHA workers' sit-in

₹15,000 monthly honorarium sought; sit-in since 17 Nov, The Hindu

In pictures

Every update

  1. November 2026
  2. 28 Nov

    ASHAs reject CM's appeal, sit-in continues

    Assembly

    On day 11, ASHA workers said they will not leave Freedom Park until an official pay order is issued. The CM urged them to return to work: 'We will keep our word.'

    Punjab Kesari (IANS) · The Hindu · Edition of Sat 28 Nov
  3. 27 Nov

    ASHA panel formed; sit-in goes on

    Assembly

    On day 10 the health department set up a committee to study higher ASHA pay. The CM said workers 'can't blackmail' the government and refused a written assurance; workers say they will stay.

    The Hans India · ThePrint (PTI) · Edition of Fri 27 Nov
  4. 26 Nov

    Five ASHA protesters taken to hospital

    Assembly

    Rain drenched about 200 ASHA workers on day nine at Freedom Park; five were hospitalised in two days. The CM promised to raise their ₹15,000 demand; they want it in writing.

    South First · Prajavani (Kannada) · Edition of Thu 26 Nov
  5. 25 Nov

    ASHA sit-in enters day eight despite CM's call

    Assembly

    ASHA workers seeking a ₹15,000 monthly honorarium stayed at Freedom Park on day eight. Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar, by video call from Japan, urged them to end the stir and promised a solution.

    The Hindu · Prajavani (Kannada) · Edition of Wed 25 Nov
  6. 24 Nov

    ASHA sit-in hits day seven; 17 hospitalised

    Assembly

    On day seven of their Freedom Park sit-in for a ₹15,000 monthly honorarium, 17 ASHA workers had been hospitalised. The CM promised to take it to Cabinet; workers want a written order.

    TV9 Kannada (Kannada) · ThePrint (PTI) · Edition of Tue 24 Nov
  7. 23 Nov

    ASHA workers' Freedom Park sit-in reaches day six

    Assembly

    ASHA health workers' sit-in at Freedom Park for a ₹15,000 monthly honorarium reached day six on 22 Nov; some were hospitalised. Chief Minister D K Shivakumar says the government is 'not rejecting' the demand.

    Vartha Bharati (Kannada) · ThePrint (PTI) · Edition of Mon 23 Nov
  8. 20 Nov

    ASHA workers stay at Freedom Park

    Assembly

    CM D K Shivakumar told protesting ASHA workers their ₹15,000 demand would go to the Cabinet and winter session, citing financial limits, and promised pending dues. Workers say they'll stay until an order is issued.

    ThePrint (PTI) · Edition of Fri 20 Nov
  9. 19 Nov

    ASHA health workers camp at Freedom Park

    Assembly

    Thousands of ASHA health workers began an indefinite protest at Bengaluru's Freedom Park on 17 Nov, seeking ₹15,000 a month instead of ₹5,000 plus incentives. The health minister promised to raise it at Thursday's Cabinet.

    The South First · Vartha Bharati (Kannada) · Edition of Thu 19 Nov
