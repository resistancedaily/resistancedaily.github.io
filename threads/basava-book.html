  2. 26 Nov

    Basava book author, publisher get interim bail

    Right upheld

    A Bengaluru court granted interim anticipatory bail on 25 Nov to author Ravi Hanj and publisher Vishweshwar Bhat. Prosecutors said the book insulted Basavanna; police say they are still seizing copies.

    Deccan Herald · The Hindu · Edition of Thu 26 Nov
  3. 24 Nov

    Held author asks court to quash FIR

    Speech

    Author Ravi Hanj asked the Karnataka High Court on 23 Nov to quash an FIR over his book, citing a ₹5 lakh bounty on his life. Police booked the man who announced it; he apologised.

    ETV Bharat (Kannada) · Samyukta Karnataka (Kannada) · Siasat (PTI) · Edition of Tue 24 Nov
  4. 23 Nov

    Author of seized Basavanna book held at airport

    Speech

    Karnataka Police took author Ravi Hanj into custody at Kochi airport on 21 Nov over his book 'Basava Mantapa', citing FIRs for promoting enmity. The Cabinet had ordered the book seized; Hanj apologised.

    Onmanorama · The News Minute · Edition of Mon 23 Nov
  5. 20 Nov

    Karnataka Cabinet orders book seized, author booked

    Speech

    After Lingayat groups protested, Karnataka's Cabinet decided on 19 Nov to seize Ravi Hanj's book 'Basava Mantapa'. Police filed an FIR against him and publisher Vishweshwar Bhat. Hanj had apologised.

    Deccan Herald · Vartha Bharati · Edition of Fri 20 Nov
