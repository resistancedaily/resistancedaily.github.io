    Siang dam opponents refuse talks until detainees freed

    Assembly

    A farmers' forum rejected the chief minister's offer of talks on 5–7 November until detainees are freed and armed forces withdrawn; a foot march heads to Itanagar. No government reply was reported.

    Arunachal Times · Arunachal Times · Scroll · Edition of Fri 6 Nov
  4. 3 Nov

    Internet cut in Upper Siang during dam shutdown

    Assembly

    A 36-hour shutdown against the proposed Siang dam began on Monday demanding detainees' release, and internet was suspended in four Upper Siang areas "to curb misinformation", Arunachal Times reports; Chief Minister Pema Khandu said "we are not talking about dam construction at this stage".

    Arunachal Times · Hub Network · Edition of Tue 3 Nov
  5. 1 Nov

    Siang dam protesters detained in Delhi

    Assembly

    Delhi Police detained protesters, including activist Katon Moyong, outside Arunachal Bhawan on Saturday as they opposed the Siang dam. Organisers say about 40 were held and allege some were sexually assaulted; some were later released. No Delhi Police response to the allegations was reported.

    Arunachal Times · Maktoob Media · ETV Bharat · Edition of Sun 1 Nov

  7. 31 Oct

    Guwahati protest backs Siang dam opponents

    Assembly

    Citizens protested outside Nehru Park in Guwahati on Friday against the Upper Siang dam in Arunachal Pradesh, demanding the release of arrested Adi leaders and protesters. Arunachal police report 69 arrests after the 23 October violence; no official response to the Guwahati protest was reported.

    Assam Tribune · Scroll · Edition of Sat 31 Oct
  8. 30 Oct

    Siang dam opponents march; 200+ get notices

    Assembly

    Opponents of the Siang dam marched on Thursday, demanding its cancellation and the release of those in custody. Police say 69 people are arrested in 53 cases and over 200 served notices; the state says current work is only feasibility surveys.

    The Federal · Bombay Samachar · Edition of Fri 30 Oct
  9. 29 Oct

    69 arrested under Arunachal anti-terror law

    Assembly

    Police say 69 people have been arrested and four detained under Arunachal's Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act over the 23 October arson in Pasighat, in 55 cases. A minister met representatives of 72 villages on the dam and said local communities' rights must stay central.

    Scroll · NE Now · Edition of Thu 29 Oct
  10. 28 Oct

    Curbs stay in Pasighat; detained activist's health worsens

    Assembly

    About 30 companies of central armed police are deployed across three Siang districts, and Pasighat's district magistrate says curbs on gatherings stay 'until further orders'. Activist Ebo Mili's family says his health is worsening in custody, where he is reportedly on hunger strike; he denies any role in the arson.

    ThePrint · Arunachal Times · Edition of Wed 28 Oct
  11. 27 Oct

    26 arrested over Pasighat violence; hunger strike

    Assembly

    East Siang police said on Monday that 26 people, five of them juveniles, are arrested over the 23 October arson in Pasighat, with eight cases filed, and urged people not to share unverified information; anti-dam activists Ebo Mili and Otul Jerang reportedly began a hunger strike in custody. Six more are held for questioning.

    India Today NE · Northeast News · India Today NE · Edition of Tue 27 Oct
  12. 26 Oct

    Curbs stay in Pasighat as CM meets groups

    Assembly

    Prohibitory orders stayed in force in parts of East Siang on Sunday; officials reported no fresh violence. Chief Minister Pema Khandu met community organisations and MLAs, blaming “misinformation” for fears over the Siang dam. Protest groups want the project scrapped, armed forces withdrawn and 10 arrested activists freed.

    New Indian Express · Edition of Mon 26 Oct
  13. 25 Oct

    Pasighat under gathering ban after dam unrest

    Assembly

    After Friday night's arson in Pasighat, police detained five youths for questioning and barred gatherings of more than five people from Saturday evening; activists including lawyer Ebo Mili surrendered, saying the bandh was peaceful. Chief Minister Pema Khandu offered talks, saying the government's doors are open.

    Arunachal Times · Maktoob Media · Edition of Sun 25 Oct
  14. 24 Oct

    Tear gas and arson during Siang dam bandh

    Assembly

    Police fired tear gas during Friday's 12-hour bandh against the Siang Upper dam project, and four to five women were hospitalised. Protesters later reportedly set fire to an NHPC office, a police station and police vehicles. Police say protesters threw stones and defied prohibitory orders. Protesters want 10 arrested activists freed.

    NE Now · EastMojo · HubNetwork · Edition of Sat 24 Oct
  15. 23 Oct

    Siang dam opponents begin 24-hour sit-in

    Assembly

    Opponents of the 11,000 MW Siang Upper Multipurpose Project began a 24-hour sit-in in Pasighat on Thursday, demanding the release of detained activists and an end to surveys; five more activists were reportedly taken into custody. The district allowed the sit-in but barred any march, and says law and order is a separate matter from dam opposition.

    DY365 · HubNetwork · Edition of Fri 23 Oct
