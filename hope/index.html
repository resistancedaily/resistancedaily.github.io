Courts · Life & safety · 03 Nov

High Court takes up killings of lawyers

Karnataka's High Court opened a public-interest case on violence against lawyers, including two murders in October, and asked the state how it handles lawyers' complaints against police.

Accountability · Life & safety · 03 Nov

Officer who fired on villagers suspended, murder case filed

Bihar police suspended an officer who fired on villagers in Madhubani, killing one, and registered a murder case the same day. He has not been arrested.

Courts · Land & livelihood · Due process · 02 Nov

Rescued bonded labourers must get aid within a week

The Supreme Court said rescued bonded labourers should ordinarily get Rs 20,000 immediate aid within seven days of their release certificate, and that pending criminal cases are no reason to withhold it.

Supreme Court · LiveLaw
Courts · Due process · 30 Oct

Pulwama man's 15-month detention quashed

The J&K High Court quashed the Public Safety Act detention of a Pulwama man held since July 2025, saying alleged use of an encrypted app was no adequate basis for detention.
