India

5 places
  1. 1Pasighat, East Siang, and Geku, Upper Siang, Arunachal PradeshAssembly · Life & safety · Land4–5women hospitalised

    Police fired tear gas during Friday's 12-hour bandh against the Siang Upper dam project, and four to five women were hospitalised. Protesters later reportedly set fire to an NHPC office, a police station and police vehicles. Police say protesters threw stones and defied prohibitory orders. Protesters want 10 arrested activists freed.

    NE Now · EastMojo · HubNetwork Photos and full story →
  2. 2Imphal and the valley districts, ManipurAssembly · Life & safety5districts

    Thousands joined a 'sound protest' in five valley districts on Friday against continuing killings, four days after three people were killed in Kangpokpi district. Police fired tear gas near the governor's residence in Imphal; no injuries were reported. The state Home Minister says three people have been held.

    Scroll · ThePrint / PTI
  3. 3Ashoka Road, New DelhiAssembly · Detention100+detained

    Police stopped a march against the Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls on Ashoka Road on Friday, short of the Election Commission's headquarters, and detained protesters. Outlook reports more than 100 detained; Scroll says several. Police had asked the marchers to leave the area; no official count was reported.

    Outlook · Scroll
  4. 4Delhi High Court, New DelhiAssembly · Due process₹10,000peace bond

    The Delhi High Court on Friday rejected student leader Neha Bora's plea against a magistrate's notice to sign a ₹10,000 bond to keep the peace. She was detained at a 16 October protest near Barakhamba Road. The court found 'adequate safeguards' and ordered that she be given all materials.

    Scroll
  5. 5Town Hall to Freedom Park, BengaluruAssembly · Expression200+marchers

    More than 200 people marched from Town Hall to Freedom Park on Friday against the electoral-roll revision; it began only after they lowered placards police objected to. The DCP said permission was conditional and covered a march with the Tricolour and constitutional placards, not a protest.

    South First

6 places
  1. 6Across SudanLife & safety306+killed by drones

    The UN human rights office says drone strikes it attributes to both the army and the RSF killed at least 306 people in the first three weeks of October, hitting homes, markets, hospitals and displacement sites. The Security Council condemned the Abyei market attack. Neither force's response was reported.

    UN News Photos and full story →
  2. 7Hmoud, Karak governorate, JordanLife & safety · Religion7killed

    Gunmen opened fire during a funeral at a church in Hmoud, Karak governorate, on Friday, killing at least seven people and wounding 34. No one has claimed the attack. Jordan's interior minister called it an 'unprecedented' terrorist attack, and the search for the gunmen continues.

    Al Jazeera / Reuters · BBC
  3. 8Trappes and La Verrière, Yvelines, FranceLife & safety1killed

    A gunman killed one person and wounded four to five others, including a 12-year-old girl, in Trappes and La Verrière on Friday. A 39-year-old suspect is in custody. Anti-terror prosecutors are examining whether victims were targeted for race or religion; the Élysée called it a 'racist, terrorist attack'.

    Al Jazeera · Guardian
  4. 9Hebron and the occupied West BankMovement · Detention · Land36arrested Thu

    Israel ordered a full West Bank lockdown after an attack in Hebron killed two Israeli soldiers; the military says it killed two gunmen. Palestinian bodies report 36 arrests and 17 attacks on olive farmers on Thursday; no Israeli comment on these was reported. The UN says closures severely restrict movement and livelihoods.

    Al Jazeera · UN News
  5. 10Taiz City, YemenLife & safety · Livelihood800,000cut off

    UN agencies say Taiz City has been cut off for 20 days by fighting between Houthi forces and government troops. WFP says more than 800,000 people are cut off from assistance; water production has halved and truck water costs have tripled. No response from either side was reported.

    UN News
  6. 11Paris and other French citiesAssembly · Due process70,000police deployed

    France is deploying about 70,000 police on Saturday and several cities have banned or rerouted protests after last weekend's rioting; 217 people have been jailed since, the justice minister says. The interior minister cited online calls for violence. Unions call the bans an abuse of authority and are challenging them in court.

    Guardian

Focus · Sudan

Sudan's long war

Since 15 April 2023, fighting between Sudan's army and the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces has killed at least 59,000 people by one count, and possibly far more, and driven about 13 million from their homes. Both sides deny targeting civilians. The war now reaches into Abyei and South Sudan.
