  1. 1DelhiAssembly · Due process2detained Fri

    Police detained Abhijeet Dipke before a Connaught Place rally and arrested him to prevent a breach of the peace; he was due before a magistrate on Saturday. Student leader Neha Bora, detained the same day, was released, and Delhi Police had not responded to Reuters.

    Reuters via Prothom Alo · Al Jazeera
  2. 2DelhiLife & safety · AssemblyClaimsof assault

    A Kerala activist says Delhi Police assaulted him, damaging his eardrum, and women's groups marched to police headquarters over alleged harassment at protests. These are allegations and no police response was found; India's government called a UN expert's report on youth retaliation 'hearsay'.

    The News Minute · The Statesman · The Hindu
  3. 3Vijayawada, Andhra PradeshLife & safety · Due process₹25 lakhcompensation

    The Andhra Pradesh High Court ordered a CBI investigation into the death of gig worker Gade Sai Krishna, 25, and compensation for his mother. A state-appointed SIT had alleged he died of police torture in May; the court also ordered disciplinary proceedings against the officers.

    The News Minute · New Indian Express
  4. 4Ukhrul–Kangpokpi, ManipurLife & safety~100killed in 2026

    ACLED data cited by NDTV counts nearly 100 deaths in 300+ incidents from 1 January to 10 October, including 19 in September. Kuki and Naga groups blame each other; accused groups deny involvement and no official inquiry has fixed responsibility.

    NDTV
  5. 5Upper Siang, ArunachalAssembly · Due process · Land4still held

    A sessions court gave interim protection from arrest to 13 people named in two cases over the Siang dam protests, while four remain in custody under the state's anti-terror law. The state government's position was not given in the report.

    Arunachal Times · Mongabay India

    The New Arab · UN News
  2. 7GazaLife & safety67%food insecure

  3. 8Baku, AzerbaijanAssembly · Expression37–200+detained, one day

    Amnesty International
