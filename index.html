Abyei market strike toll rises to 153

Abyei's caretaker information minister says Monday's drone strike on Amiet Market killed at least 153 people, up from 73, with fires still burning; MSF is treating more than 20 wounded. Tear gas in Imphal; Kuki man shot dead

Police fired tear gas on Wednesday as protesters over Monday's killing of three Meitei firewood collectors tried to enter an Imphal event attended by the Chief Minister; three people had minor injuries. A Kuki man, Paolen Khongsai, was shot dead on Tuesday. Police have arrested three suspects in Monday's ambush.

New Delhi · Due process · Assembly7

Jantar Mantar pair freed; UN experts seek probe

A Delhi magistrate on Wednesday ordered the release of Saurav Das and Ashutosh Ranka, jailed over a Jantar Mantar protest, rejecting a police request for three more days' custody. On Tuesday two dozen UN experts urged an independent inquiry into force used at the protests; India has said police acted strictly in accordance with the law.

Race Course area, New Delhi · Land · Due process6

Century-old Delhi mosque told to vacate

The Land and Development Office told the Race Course mosque near the Prime Minister's residence to vacate within six days, calling it unauthorised on government land; the Delhi Waqf Board says it is Waqf property notified in 1977. Nearly 700 nearby families were displaced; police say they were given homes in Mundka.

Sudan's long war

Since 15 April 2023, fighting between Sudan's army and the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces has killed at least 59,000 people by one count, and possibly far more, and driven about 13 million from their homes. Both sides deny targeting civilians. The war now reaches into Abyei and South Sudan.
