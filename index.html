Villages along the Siang oppose an 11,000 MW dam that would submerge 27 villages. Four villagers have been detained under an anti-terror law, and gatherings are banned.

Focus · Sudan

Sudan's long war

Since 15 April 2023, fighting between Sudan's army and the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces has killed at least 59,000 people by one count, and possibly far more, and driven about 13 million from their homes. Both sides deny targeting civilians. The war now reaches into Abyei and South Sudan.

How we report
  • We cover rights, not parties: life, assembly, expression, press, due process, land.
  • Every number links to its source, with ground reporting first.
  • Official positions appear next to allegations; claims are labelled as claims.
  • When sources disagree, we show the range. Maps show land only, with no political borders.
  • Photos are freely licensed and credited. "File photo" marks an older picture of the place, not of the event. We blur protesters' and children's faces.
