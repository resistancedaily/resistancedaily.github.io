4 places · Killings, injuries, attacks on civilians

    Shutdown in Manipur valley after villagers killed

    Markets, schools and offices shut across five valley districts on Tuesday in a 48-hour strike after three Meitei villagers were shot dead on Monday; organisers demand arrests within 48 hours. Officials said extra forces were deployed, and police reported no incidents.

    Also: AssemblyNational Herald · Imphal Times
  • Gaza City and Khan Younis, Gaza4 killed Tuesday

    Couple and teenager among four killed in Gaza

    Medics said an Israeli airstrike killed a couple in their Gaza City apartment on Tuesday, and gunfire killed a 17-year-old in Khan Younis and one person in Zeitoun. Israel's military said it struck a militant in Gaza City and did not immediately comment on the shootings.

    Reuters via US News · Al Jazeera
  • 80–150reported killed
    Al-Dabab, West Kordofan, Sudan

    Drone strike near Kordofan market kills dozens

    A drone hit a transport hub near the market in al-Dabab, an RSF-held town, on Sunday evening, killing 80 people according to residents and medics, or about 150 according to local groups. The RSF-aligned Tasis alliance blamed an army drone; the reports carry no army response.

    Al Jazeera · Radio Dabanga
  • 27+civilians killed
    Magwe, Sagaing, Mandalay, Rakhine and Chin, Myanmar

    Airstrikes kill at least 27 civilians in Myanmar

    Military airstrikes from 16 to 18 October killed at least 27 civilians in five regions and states, The Irrawaddy reports, including 12 to 15 people at a WiFi shop in Yesagyo. The regime's defence minister called the operations steps toward peace.

    The Irrawaddy

Due process

2 places · Detention, charges, courts, legal access
  • New Delhi8 days held

    Jailed Jantar Mantar protesters move High Court

    Saurav Das and Ashutosh Ranka, held since 13 October for entering the closed Jantar Mantar protest site, asked the Delhi High Court on Tuesday for an urgent hearing, saying their custody breaches their right to liberty. The magistrate who extended it found their sureties unsatisfactory and said they could breach the peace.

    Also: AssemblyBar & Bench · The Wire · Bar & Bench (magistrate's order)

    Iran arrests dozens more over winter protests

    Qazvin's intelligence directorate said it arrested 39 people over the winter anti-government protests (VOA's headline says 38), accusing them of arson, looting and killing security personnel. Amnesty International and Human Rights Watch have called similar charges against protesters fabricated.

    Over 1,100 families evicted in Assam forest drive

    Authorities evicted more than 1,100 families, mostly Bengali-speaking Muslims, from forest land over two days; residents say they lived there for decades and that more than 10 mosques were demolished.
