# Resistance Daily: daily posting checklist (the human part)

**Status: first draft, 3 Nov 2026. The owner is still correcting it.**

The scheduled routine (see `ROUTINE.md`) does the research, publishes the website, makes the slides and writes every
caption. This checklist covers what a **person** does after that: posting to Instagram, Facebook, X and WhatsApp, and
leaving reach comments. Follow it top to bottom. You don't need to decide anything that isn't written here.

Things marked **[confirm]** are guesses about the owner's current habits or about the Instagram app. Fix them when you
know the answer.

## Step 1. Read the notification and check the site

1. Open the notification. Its first line is the day's biggest story. Near the end it says:
   - `Site: ... (pushed)` = the website is updated. Continue.
   - `NOT pushed: <reason>` = the website is **not** updated. Don't post anything that says "full edition: link in
     bio" until it is fixed. Open the routine's session in the Claude app and ask it to push the edition.
   - Any other step that says it failed: read what failed, then post only what is ready.
2. Open **https://resistancedaily.github.io/** on your phone.
   - [ ] The "Today" box shows **today's date**.
   - [ ] Tap "Read today's full edition". Photos load and nothing looks broken.
   - [ ] If a Focus story was updated, tap the "In focus" card. The page opens and shows "What's new" with today's date.
3. If anything is wrong, don't post yet. Tell the routine's session what you saw (a screenshot helps).

## Step 2. Save today's files

The routine sends files into its session in the Claude app. Daily files:

| File | What it's for | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| `DATE-slide1.jpg` … `DATE-slide6.jpg` | The 6 carousel images | Instagram post, Reel |
| `slide-captions.txt` | The caption for each slide (Slide 1 = the post caption, with hashtags) | Instagram post |
| `alt-text.txt` | Alt text for each slide | Instagram post (accessibility) |
| `narration.txt` | What to say over each slide in the Reel | Reel voice-over |
| `reel-caption.txt` | The single caption for the Reel | Reel |
| `x-post.txt` | The X post, already under 280 characters | X |
| `whatsapp.txt` | The WhatsApp Channel post | WhatsApp |
| `reach.txt` | Comment drafts for other outlets' posts | Step 9 |

Extra files on some days:

| File | When | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Focus `...-slide1.jpg` etc., with their own `slide-captions.txt`, `alt-text.txt`, `narration.txt`, `reel-caption.txt` | A new Focus carousel or a big update | Step 4 |
| `story.txt` | A Focus had a small update | Step 5 (Instagram Story) |
| `old-caption-line.txt` | A Focus or story changed | Step 5 (edit the old post) |

You won't get `caption.txt` or `facebook-post.txt`, and you don't need them.

1. [ ] Save the 6 slide images to your phone's Photos **in order** (slide 1 first). Tip: make an album named with the
       date, e.g. `RD 2026-11-03`, so the Instagram picker shows only today's slides.
2. [ ] Open each .txt file once in the Claude app or a notes app so you can copy from it. Keep `slide-captions.txt`
       and `alt-text.txt` handy.
3. [ ] Look at all 6 slides: right date on slide 1, no text cut off, no broken photo. If something is wrong, stop and
       tell the routine's session.

## Step 3. Post the daily carousel on Instagram

1. Open Instagram as **@resistance.daily**. Tap **+** → **Post**.
2. Tap the **select multiple** icon. Tap slides **1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 in that order**. The little numbers on the
   thumbnails must read 1–6 in slide order.
3. Keep the **4:5 portrait** shape. The slides are 1080×1350. If the preview looks cropped to a square, tap the
   expand/resize icon at the bottom left of the preview. Check that the date and text on slide 1 aren't cut.
4. Tap **Next**. **No filters, no edits.** Tap **Next** again.
5. **Caption:** copy the text under `===== Slide 1 =====` in `slide-captions.txt` and paste it as the caption. It
   already has the summary, the sources line and the hashtags at the end.
   - Don't add extra hashtags and don't tag other accounts in the caption.
   - **Slides 2–5 captions:** `slide-captions.txt` also has a short caption for each story slide (slide 6 has none).
     **[confirm]** where these go in the app. If Instagram on your phone shows a caption box per photo, paste each
     one there. If it doesn't, leave them out: the post caption already summarises all four stories.
6. **Tag people:** skip.
7. **Add location:** skip for the daily carousel (it covers many places). For a Focus post, see Step 4.
8. **Alt text** (this makes the post readable for blind and low-vision people):
   - On the caption screen tap **Advanced settings** (or **More options**) → **Accessibility** → **Write alt text**.
     Instagram moves this menu now and then. **[confirm the current path]**
   - Each slide shows a box. Paste the matching line from `alt-text.txt` (`Slide 1: …` into the first box, and so on).
     Paste only the text after `Slide N:`.
   - Tap **Done / Save**.
9. **Share to Facebook:** turn this **on**, so Facebook gets the same post. We don't post to Facebook separately.
10. Tap **Share**.
11. When it's up, open the post and swipe through once. All 6 slides should be there, in order.

## Step 4. Focus carousel (only when you get Focus slide files)

Same as Step 3, with these differences:

1. Use the **Focus** images and that folder's `slide-captions.txt` and `alt-text.txt` (not the daily ones).
2. **Caption:** paste the Slide 1 caption (it starts with "Focus · …", or "Update · …" for an update carousel).
3. **Add location:** **yes**. Use the Focus story's main place, which is written above the title on the Focus page
   (e.g. "Upper Siang, Arunachal Pradesh"). Pick the closest match Instagram offers (district or state). Skip it if
   nothing sensible comes up.
4. After posting, add the post to that story's **Highlight** if it has one. **[confirm: are carousels added to
   Highlights, or only Stories?]**
5. If you also got `old-caption-line.txt`, do Step 5.2 for the previous post on that story.

## Step 5. Updates: Story and old-caption line

The notification tells you which applies: `Instagram: Story`, `update carousel` or `correction`. Today (3 Nov) it was
"Story for Siang and Sudan".

### 5.1 Instagram Story (small update)

`story.txt` looks like this:

```
<Title> · update DATE
<one line on what's new>
Full story: tap the link
Link sticker: https://resistancedaily.github.io/focus/SLUG.html
```

1. Instagram → **+** → **Story**.
2. Background: a photo from that Focus story (the Focus page's top photo, or its slide 1 if you have one), or a plain
   colour. **[confirm the owner's usual background]**
3. Add text: the first three lines of `story.txt`, as written.
4. Tap the **sticker** icon → **Link** → paste the URL from the `Link sticker:` line. Set the sticker text to
   "Full story" if the app lets you.
5. Share to **Your story**.
6. Open the Story → **Highlight** → add it to **that story's Highlight** (e.g. "Siang"). If there is no Highlight yet,
   create one named after the story.

One `story.txt` per Focus. If two Focus stories changed (e.g. Siang and Sudan), post two Stories.

### 5.2 Edit the previous post's caption (`old-caption-line.txt`)

We never delete old posts. Each one is a dated record. We add one line at the top instead.

1. Find the **most recent earlier post** on that story (a Focus carousel or a daily post that covered it).
2. **⋯** → **Edit** → put the cursor at the very **start** of the caption.
3. Paste the line from `old-caption-line.txt`, then a blank line. It starts with `Update DATE:` (or `Correction DATE:`
   when the old post was wrong).
4. Tap **Done**.

## Step 6. Reel (when you have time)

The Reel is the same carousel as a short video, about 5 seconds per slide, with a calm voice-over.

1. Instagram → **+** → **Reel** → select the 6 slides in order.
2. Set each clip to **5 seconds** (the whole Reel is about 30 seconds).
3. Record the voice-over from `narration.txt`:
   - One line per slide, read **slowly and calmly**. Each line is about 4.5 seconds. Pause where you see ` …`.
   - Read numbers the way they're written in the file (e.g. "sixteen").
   - The last line ends with "link in bio". No pause after it.
   - If a line runs over its slide, read slower and cut nothing. Don't speed up.
4. No music, or very quiet background only. **[confirm]**
5. **Caption:** paste all of `reel-caption.txt`.
6. **Alt text:** if the Reel offers it, use the Slide 1 line from `alt-text.txt`. **[confirm]**
7. **Share to Facebook:** on. Tap **Share**.

For a Focus or update carousel, use that folder's `narration.txt` and `reel-caption.txt`.

## Step 9. Reach comments (spread through the day)

`reach.txt` has 6–10 comment drafts, in priority order. **Stop wherever you like.** Rules:

- **Never more than 2 comments under the same outlet's posts in one day.**
- **Spread them out** (for example 2 in the morning, 2 at lunch, 2 in the evening). Many comments in a few minutes
  look like spam to Instagram and YouTube and can get the account restricted.
- Post each one **by hand** from the Resistance Daily account. Never copy-paste the same text twice.

For each entry:

1. Look at the platform line (YOUTUBE / X / INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK).
2. **If there's a direct link** (YouTube, X): open it and check the post really is about that story.
3. **If it says "look for"** (Instagram, Facebook): open that outlet's account and find the post described ("@outlet's
   post on <story>, around <date>"). **If you can't find a post that matches, skip this entry.** Entries marked
   "post not opened: check it matches" need this check.
4. Paste the comment. Small edits so it fits the post are fine. Keep it factual and neutral: no emoji, no hashtags, no
   "follow us".
5. Under posts about deaths or attacks, keep the comment to the facts only.
6. Post it. **[optional]** Note which ones you posted, so you don't pick the same outlet for the same story tomorrow.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| No notification by about 10:00 IST | Open the routine in the Claude app and check whether it ran. If not, run it by hand or post nothing that day. **[confirm the owner's preference]** |
| Notification says "NOT pushed" | The site isn't updated. Don't post. Ask the routine's session to push, then start again at Step 1. |
| A slide has a typo or a wrong fact | Don't post. Tell the routine's session exactly what's wrong and ask for corrected slides. |
| You spot a mistake after posting | Don't delete the post. Ask the routine for a correction. It will give you a `Correction DATE:` line for the old caption (Step 5.2). |
| A reach-comment post can't be found | Skip that entry. Never guess an account. |
| Instagram crops the slides to a square | Go back to the picker and tap the expand icon before Next (Step 3.3). |
