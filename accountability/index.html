CustodyPower: Prison warders

Inquiry into alleged torture after Theni jailbreak

Theni district jail, Tamil Nadu · 26 Oct 2026 · Governed by TVK-led coalition

Seventeen remand prisoners escaped from Theni district jail and were later caught; one alleged in a video that warders tortured them and denied them food and water. On 2 November the State Legal Services Authority ordered an inquiry, including whether the prisoners have since been threatened or coerced.

Official version: The Chief Minister set up a high-level committee to strengthen prison security; no response to the torture allegations was reported.

How cases are chosen
  • A clear power gap: police or officials, elected leaders and their supporters, employers, landlords, or a dominant group over a weaker one.
  • A named source and an official step (an FIR, a suspension or a court notice). No bare accusations.
  • Outside India, only cases where an accountability process (a charge, a prosecution, an inquiry) is reported and can be followed; at most about a third of open cases.
  • The accused are described by role, and named only once police or a court have named them. The police or official version sits beside every allegation.
  • Sexual-assault survivors and child victims are never identifiable. No photos of bodies.
  • Every case is re-checked 30, 90 and 180 days after the incident. If nothing is reported, we say so, with the date.
  • Days are counted from the incident to 03 Nov 2026.
