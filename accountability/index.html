Rio de Janeiro · BrazilPolice force

Rio raid killed 121; no one charged over the deaths

  1. ✓ Inquiry opened
  2. ✓ Officers charged
  3. Charges over the deaths
  4. Verdict

373 days since the incident

Details

A police operation against a drug gang in Rio's Penha and Alemão favelas left 121 people dead, four of them police, the deadliest in Brazil's history. A year on, inquiries into the deaths, including a decapitated body and suspected torture and executions, have not concluded.

Official version: Police called it a mega-operation against the Comando Vermelho gang.   • Inquiry opened: done (state prosecutors and Federal Police)
  • Officers charged: done (27, for theft and abuses, not killings)
  • Charges over the deaths: not yet
  • Verdict: not yet

Penha and Alemão favelas, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. Power: State police.

How cases are chosen
  • A clear power gap: police or officials, elected leaders and their supporters, employers, landlords, or a dominant group over a weaker one.
  • A named source and an official step (an FIR, a suspension or a court notice). No bare accusations.
  • Outside India, only cases where an accountability process (a charge, a prosecution, an inquiry) is reported and can be followed; at most about a third of open cases.
  • The accused are described by role, and named only once police or a court have named them. The police or official version sits beside every allegation.
  • Sexual-assault survivors and child victims are never identifiable. No photos of bodies.
  • Every case is re-checked 30, 90 and 180 days after the incident. If nothing is reported, we say so, with the date.
  • Days are counted from the incident to 05 Nov 2026. Step track: ✓ done · ? not reported · plain: not reached yet.
