India

3 places
  1. 1Pasighat, East Siang, Arunachal PradeshAssembly · Land · Due process10activists held

    Prohibitory orders stayed in force in parts of East Siang on Sunday; officials reported no fresh violence. Chief Minister Pema Khandu met community organisations and MLAs, blaming “misinformation” for fears over the Siang dam. Protest groups want the project scrapped, armed forces withdrawn and 10 arrested activists freed.

    New Indian Express Photos and full story →
  2. 2Jamia Millia Islamia, New DelhiAssembly · Expression · Education2006last union

    Police kept students out of Jamia's locked campus on Sunday and detained some; police say two women held outside were released and a man was put in preventive detention. Students later ended their two-week protest after the university agreed to hold union elections, absent since 2006, and withdraw suspensions.

    The Hindu · Outlook
  3. 3Bengaluru and across KarnatakaVote · Due process61,059deletion forms

    Election officials themselves filed at least 61,059 forms to delete names from Karnataka's rolls, none stating the reason the rules require, The Hindu found; most came after the 23 September deadline. Officials say the system auto-generated them when voters could not be reached. The final roll is due 27 October.

    The Hindu

World

7 places
  1. 4Kordofan, Darfur and Blue Nile, SudanLife & safety306killed by drones

    The UN human rights office says drone strikes killed at least 306 people in Sudan in the first three weeks of October, including 139 when a truck was hit at Dibab, West Kordofan, on 18 October. It attributes strikes to both the army and the RSF; neither side's response was reported.

    Radio Tamazuj · Dabanga Photos and full story →
  2. 5Gaza City and Khan Younis, GazaLife & safety · Education8killed

    Israeli strikes killed at least eight people across Gaza on Sunday, including three security guards at the Islamic University in Gaza City; seven female students were hurt in an earlier strike on the campus. Gaza's Health Ministry counts 1,551 killed since the ceasefire. No Israeli military comment was reported.

    Al Jazeera · Middle East Eye
  3. 6Hebron, occupied West BankDisplacement · Life & safety2,670displaced

    More than 2,670 people, mostly women, children and elderly people, have been displaced by Israel's Hebron operation, the Palestinian prime minister's office said on Sunday, citing the municipality. Troops ordered residents of Ein Deir Baha to leave. Israel's public broadcaster reports the army will end the operation within days.

    Times of Israel · Middle East Eye
  4. 7Binnari, Karim Lamido, Taraba State, NigeriaLife & safety10–40killed

    Amnesty International says at least 40 people were killed when armed men attacked Binnari village on Friday, burning homes, shops and food; police had earlier confirmed 10 deaths, and many people are missing. Police say officers were deployed to restore order; no group has been named.

    Premium Times
  5. 8Bafwasende, Tshopo province, DR CongoLife & safety · Health4,700+Ebola deaths

    A nurse was killed and another health worker badly injured on Saturday in Bafwasende, Tshopo province, while responding to an Ebola alert, the WHO says. Local authorities have not commented. The outbreak, Congo's deadliest, has recorded more than 9,800 cases and 4,700 deaths since May.

    AP via Newsday
  6. 9Paris, Rome and other cities in France and ItalyAssembly · Life & safety1,314arrests in France

    France reported 1,314 arrests at Saturday's cost-of-living protests, with 53 officers hurt and two people seriously injured; the interior minister blamed “radical activists”. Italy arrested nine and took 371 people, 110 of them minors, for checks; its prime minister said peaceful protest must be protected.

    BBC via AOL · News On AIR
  7. 10Cheras, Kuala Lumpur, MalaysiaDue process · Life & safety12days in custody

    Malaysia's police chief ordered the CID to investigate the death of Noorizwan Zulkefly, 42, who died on 1 October after 12 days in Cheras police custody, citing unusual injuries. Family lawyers say an autopsy found brain bleeding from head trauma; police still class it as sudden death.

    Malaysiakini · Free Malaysia Today

Sudan's long war

Since 15 April 2023, fighting between Sudan's army and the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces has killed at least 59,000 people by one count, and possibly far more, and driven about 13 million from their homes. Both sides deny targeting civilians. The war now reaches into Abyei and South Sudan.
