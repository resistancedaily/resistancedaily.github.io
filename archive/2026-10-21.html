    Markets, schools and offices stayed shut across five valley districts on Tuesday in a 48-hour strike called by the Native People Committee Manipur after three Meitei villagers were shot dead in the Sunurok hills on Monday; the committee demanded arrests within 48 hours.

    National Herald · Imphal Times
  • 4Al-Dabab, West Kordofan, Sudan80–150 reported killed

    A drone struck a transport hub near the market in al-Dabab, an RSF-held town in West Kordofan, on Sunday evening as villagers gathered for rides home, killing 80 people according to residents and medics, while local groups cited by Radio Dabanga put the toll at about 150.

    Al Jazeera · Radio Dabanga
  • 5Gaza City and Khan Younis, Gaza4 killed Tuesday

    Medics said an Israeli airstrike killed a husband and wife in their Gaza City apartment on Tuesday, and Israeli gunfire killed a 17-year-old in Khan Younis and another person in Zeitoun; the Israeli military said it struck a militant in Gaza City and did not immediately comment on the shootings.

    Reuters via US News · Al Jazeera
  • 6Magwe, Sagaing, Mandalay, Rakhine and Chin, Myanmar27+ civilians killed

    The Irrawaddy reports that military airstrikes from 16 to 18 October killed at least 27 civilians and injured 11 in five regions and states, including a strike on a Starlink WiFi shop in Yesagyo township that killed 12 people by the opposition NUG's count and 15 by local media's.

    The Irrawaddy

  • We cover rights, not parties: life, assembly, expression, press, due process, land.

  • Official positions appear next to allegations; claims are labelled as claims.
  • When sources disagree, we show the range. Maps show land only, with no political borders.
  • Photos are freely licensed and credited. "File photo" marks an older picture of the place, not of the event. We blur protesters' faces.
