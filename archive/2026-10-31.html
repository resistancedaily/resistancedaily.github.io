India

3 places
  1. 1Supreme Court, New DelhiDue process · Detention4.5 yrsheld before bail

    The Supreme Court on Friday dismissed the NIA's challenge to bail for Kashmiri activist Khurram Parvez in a UAPA terror-funding case, citing his long incarceration since his November 2021 arrest. The High Court had granted bail in July after nearly four and a half years; the NIA's arguments were not reported.

    LiveLaw
  2. 2Hulimangala, near Bengaluru, KarnatakaLife & safety · Due process19arrested

    Members of the group Rashtra Rakshana Pade entered a shed housing labourers in Hulimangala, near Bengaluru, on 28 October, accusing them of being illegal Bangladeshi migrants, and a fight followed. Police arrested 19 people from both sides on complaints and counter-complaints; a police release says the workers maintain they are from West Bengal.

    Deccan Chronicle
  3. 3Guwahati, AssamAssembly · Land69Arunachal arrests

    Citizens protested outside Nehru Park in Guwahati on Friday against the Upper Siang dam in Arunachal Pradesh, demanding the release of arrested Adi leaders and protesters. Arunachal police report 69 arrests after the 23 October violence; no official response to the Guwahati protest was reported.

  1. 4Darfur, SudanLife & safety25named

    The Centre for Information Resilience, a UK open-source group, says it has reasonable grounds to believe 25 commanders of the RSF, the army and allied forces were directly or indirectly responsible for sexual violence at five sites, including El Fasher and Gezira. Neither force responded to Al Jazeera; both have denied such abuses.

  2. 5Gaza City, GazaLife & safety · Shelter800displaced

    The UN rights office said on Friday Israeli military zones in Gaza are expanding west, pushing Palestinians into smaller areas; a 25 October drone strike and fire at tents in Al Shujaiyeh displaced about 800 people. Israel has said it aims to deploy across 70 per cent of Gaza; no Israeli comment was reported.

    UN News
  3. 6Occupied West BankLife & safety · Livelihood2arrested

    Settlers beat a Palestinian man in Burqa and attacked olive pickers and a foreign activist in Kafr Malik on Friday, where soldiers arrested two Palestinian harvesters, according to reports citing Wafa; troops fired sound grenades at pickers in Al-Lubban Al-Sharqiya. No Israeli military comment was reported.

    TRT World
  4. 7Moscow, RussiaPress freedom9 Novlast day

    Novaya Gazeta, Russia's best-known independent newspaper, said on Friday it will close on 9 November because authorities and security services forced it to; it reports being evicted from its Moscow office. UN rights chief Volker Türk called it a devastating blow to independent media. No Kremlin comment was reported.

    Al Jazeera · Meduza · UN News
  5. 8Kathmandu, NepalPress freedom · Due process2+detained

    Plainclothes police in Kathmandu took at least two journalists, Bishwamani Subedi of Nepal Press and editor Shovaraj Baral, for questioning on Friday without showing warrants or stating grounds. The Prime Minister's office said it had no prior knowledge and ordered their release; the Press Council demanded the legal basis.

    Kathmandu Post
  6. 9Eastern DR CongoLife & safety · Livelihood28.8Macutely hungry

    About 28.8 million people in DR Congo, 23 per cent of those analysed, face high acute food insecurity through December, a new IPC alert says, with North Kivu, South Kivu and Ituri hardest hit. It cites conflict, displacement, an Ebola outbreak and falling aid. No government response was reported.

    UN News

Focus · Sudan

Sudan's long war

Since 15 April 2023, fighting between Sudan's army and the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces has killed at least 59,000 people by one count, and possibly far more, and driven about 13 million from their homes. Both sides deny targeting civilians. The war now reaches into Abyei and South Sudan.
