Report something

Seen a right denied, or upheld, where you live? Tell us. We publish only what we can source, so we may not use every report, but every one is read.

Before you write

  • Your safety first. Do not send from a work or shared device or account if the report could put you at risk.
  • No names of survivors of sexual violence, or of children. We never publish them; please do not send them.
  • What we need to publish: a published source (a news report, court order, FIR number or official statement) or a way to find one. A report without one becomes a lead we try to verify.
  • We never name you unless you ask us to. Say if we may write back.

Nothing is sent from this page: the button opens a draft in your own email app, addressed to resistancedaily.in@gmail.com, and you press send.
