Right upheld Palestinian journalist freed after five days uncharged

Israel released journalist Abed Abou Shhadeh after five days' detention without charge on suspicion of "contact with a foreign agent". His lawyers at Adalah said police had no evidence; no official comment was reported.

4 Nov 2026

Ethiopia suspends one of its oldest newspapers

Ethiopia's Media Authority indefinitely suspended the Reporter, the last private Amharic newspaper, on 2 November, accusing it of content against "national interests"; HRW calls it part of a wider media crackdown.

Addis Ababa, Ethiopia · HRW
3 Nov 2026

Sudan law puts journalists at risk, CPJ says

CPJ said on Monday that a 19 October change to Article 51 of Sudan's Criminal Act, criminalising online or media support for threats to state security, could bring journalists life in prison or death; the journalists' syndicate warned of prosecutions, and the army did not answer CPJ.

Sudan · CPJ
1 Nov 2026

Journalist's website taken offline by host

Hosting firm Hostinger suspended journalist Paranjoy Guha Thakurta's website on 28 October over a "Fraud/Scam" abuse report, days after a court ordered one 2019 article removed in a defamation suit. In a statement on Saturday he called it backdoor censorship; Hostinger support said suspension was "not negotiable".

New Delhi · Maktoob Media
31 Oct 2026

Novaya Gazeta says it is forced to close

Novaya Gazeta, Russia's best-known independent newspaper, said on Friday it will close on 9 November because authorities and security services forced it to; it reports being evicted from its Moscow office. UN rights chief Volker Türk called it a devastating blow to independent media. No Kremlin comment was reported.

Moscow, Russia · Al Jazeera · Meduza · UN News
31 Oct 2026

Police detain journalists without warrants

Plainclothes police in Kathmandu took at least two journalists, Bishwamani Subedi of Nepal Press and editor Shovaraj Baral, for questioning on Friday without showing warrants or stating grounds. The Prime Minister's office said it had no prior knowledge and ordered their release; the Press Council demanded the legal basis.

Kathmandu, Nepal · Kathmandu Post
30 Oct 2026

Activists attacked by settlers are arrested, banned

Israeli police arrested two activist-journalists after footage showed settlers, one armed, attacking them near Umm al-Kheir overnight Wednesday; they were released but banned from the West Bank for the rest of the year. Police cite an alleged attempt to steal weapons; no settlers were reported arrested.

Umm al-Kheir, South Hebron Hills, occupied West Bank · Times of Israel
29 Oct 2026

Egypt court orders Mada Masr shut

A Cairo court ordered the independent outlet Mada Masr closed and fined editor Lina Attalah 3 million Egyptian pounds for running an unlicensed website. She was held for hours until the fine was paid and faces a retrial on 11 November over a 2024 sentence. No official comment was reported.

Cairo, Egypt · Al Jazeera · The National
23 Oct 2026

Settler suspects freed; olive harvest attacks continue

A Jerusalem court released nine of ten settlers held over assaults on a Washington Post photojournalist and European diplomats to house arrest; police say one remains in custody. On Thursday settlers burned olive trees near Imatin and stole olives near Yabad, Wafa reports. UN officials said attacks on olive farmers 'must stop'.

Jerusalem and the West Bank · Al Jazeera · UN News
23 Oct 2026

Georgian journalist arrested on treason charge

Georgia's Prosecutor's Office arrested Domenti Chubinidze of the newspaper Asaval-Dasavali on a treason (sabotage) charge over his reporting on the David Gareji monastery complex; it faces up to four years. Prosecutors say he acted against the state's interests and will seek pretrial detention.

Tbilisi, Georgia · Georgia Today

UN experts warn of olive harvest attacks

UN experts urged Israel on Monday to protect Palestinian olive farmers, as OCHA reported five Palestinians killed and more than 240 injured by Israeli forces or settlers in two weeks, with 160 harvest-related settler attacks; Israeli police said they arrested 10 suspects after weekend attacks on a US photojournalist and European diplomats. Rights groups say attacks on Palestinians rarely lead to arrests or punishment.

West Bank · UN News · AP via Boston Herald
