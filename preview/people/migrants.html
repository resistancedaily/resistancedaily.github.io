Thirteen migrants die as boat sinks off Crete

At least 13 people died when a migrant boat sank off Crete; the coastguard rescued 18, all Sudanese. Authorities said the boat had likely been at sea for days in strong winds.

Off Crete, Greece · BBC
9 Nov 2026

Migrant boat sinks off Senegal, killing 34

A pirogue carrying migrants capsized off northwestern Senegal on Saturday, killing at least 34, the state press agency said. The interior ministry said one person was rescued and searches continue.

Off the Lompoul dunes, Kébémer, Senegal · Al Jazeera (AFP, Reuters)
8 Nov 2026

First winter storm floods Gaza's tent camps

The season's first storm flooded about 70,000 tents of displaced families, Gaza's media office said; aid groups blame Israeli limits on shelter materials. No Israeli response was reported.

Deir el-Balah, Gaza City and Khan Younis, Gaza · Al Jazeera
4 Nov 2026

61 alleged undocumented migrants detained in Ahmedabad

Police detained 61 people, including women and children, in Ahmedabad on Monday as alleged undocumented immigrants, mostly Bangladeshi, they say; officials say deportation proceedings are under way.

Ahmedabad, Gujarat · Scroll
2 Nov 2026

Blue Nile's displaced short of food, medicine

Displaced people in Sudan's Blue Nile state told Radio Tamazuj on Sunday that aid falls short as food and medicines run low and disease spreads. The state aid commissioner puts the displaced at about 27,400 (Dabanga reports the figure as families) and says WFP and others are delivering food, cash and hygiene kits.

Blue Nile state, Sudan · Radio Tamazuj · Dabanga
2 Nov 2026

Hebron operation has displaced almost 5,000

Al Jazeera reported on Sunday that almost 5,000 people have been displaced since Israel's military operation in Hebron began on 23 October, after two Israeli soldiers were shot. UNICEF counts over 480 families displaced, including more than 1,800 children. Israel's defence minister says residents cannot return until operations end.

Hebron, occupied West Bank · Al Jazeera · UNICEF via ReliefWeb · AP via KATV
31 Oct 2026

Vigilante group confronts migrant workers; 19 held

Members of the group Rashtra Rakshana Pade entered a shed housing labourers in Hulimangala, near Bengaluru, on 28 October, accusing them of being illegal Bangladeshi migrants, and a fight followed. Police arrested 19 people from both sides on complaints and counter-complaints; a police release says the workers maintain they are from West Bengal.

Hulimangala, near Bengaluru, Karnataka · Deccan Chronicle
31 Oct 2026

UN: Gazans pushed into ever smaller areas

The UN rights office said on Friday Israeli military zones in Gaza are expanding west, pushing Palestinians into smaller areas; a 25 October drone strike and fire at tents in Al Shujaiyeh displaced about 800 people. Israel has said it aims to deploy across 70 per cent of Gaza; no Israeli comment was reported.

Gaza City, Gaza · UN News
29 Oct 2026

Hebron operation now affects about 5,000 people

About 780 families, roughly 5,000 people, have been displaced or are trapped at home as Israel's Hebron operation entered its sixth day, the city's municipality says. The army began it after two soldiers were killed on Friday; no Israeli comment on the figures was reported.

Hebron, occupied West Bank · Times of Israel · The New Arab
26 Oct 2026

Over 2,670 displaced in Hebron, Palestinian officials say

More than 2,670 people, mostly women, children and elderly people, have been displaced by Israel's Hebron operation, the Palestinian prime minister's office said on Sunday, citing the municipality. Troops ordered residents of Ein Deir Baha to leave. Israel's public broadcaster reports the army will end the operation within days.

Hebron, occupied West Bank · Times of Israel · Middle East Eye
25 Oct 2026

Imphal sit-in against any division of Manipur

Five civil society groups led by AMUCO held a sit-in in Imphal East on Saturday, starting a statewide agitation against any administrative division of Manipur; their six demands include repeal of AFSPA and rehabilitation of displaced people. No police action or government response was reported.

Imphal East, Manipur · NE Now
25 Oct 2026

Families displaced in Hebron military operation

About 250 families, almost 1,200 people according to Hebron's municipality, were displaced as Israeli forces entered Hebron on Saturday after two soldiers were killed; demolitions began near the attack site. The military says it is dismantling "terrorist infrastructure" and removing structures that pose a security threat.

Hebron, occupied West Bank · The New Arab (AFP) · Times of Israel
22 Oct 2026

Century-old Delhi mosque told to vacate

The Land and Development Office told the Race Course mosque near the Prime Minister's residence to vacate within six days, calling it unauthorised on government land; the Delhi Waqf Board says it is Waqf property notified in 1977. Nearly 700 nearby families were displaced; police say they were given homes in Mundka.

Race Course area, New Delhi · Scroll · ETV Bharat · Siasat
20 Oct 2026

Strikes kill four in Gaza, including two teens

Hospitals said Israeli strikes killed at least two people in Gaza City and two 17-year-olds in Khan Younis on Monday; the Israeli military said it struck Hamas fighters in Gaza City and did not comment on Khan Younis. OCHA counts 57 Palestinians killed between 29 September and 12 October, and about 950,000 displaced people living in sites at risk of winter flooding.

Gaza City and Khan Younis, Gaza · AP via Boston Herald · UN News
19 Oct 2026

RSF accused of killing 11 displaced people

A Darfur regional health minister says an RSF attack on a displacement site in Sirba locality on Saturday killed 11 people, mostly women and children, and injured five; local outlets describe a drone strike. The RSF did not immediately comment.

Sirba, West Darfur, Sudan · Anadolu · Dabanga
