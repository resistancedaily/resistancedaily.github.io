Dhubri eviction drive displaces over 4,000 people

Authorities in Assam's Dhubri district demolished the homes of more than 640 Bengali Muslim families on Sunday, displacing over 4,000 people, officials told Scroll. The Union government told the Delhi High Court on Thursday it will take no coercive action for four weeks against the Race Course mosque near the Prime Minister's residence while it decides a Waqf Board representation. Over 1,100 families evicted in Assam forest drive
