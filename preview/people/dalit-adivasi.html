Dalit man killed; wife's brother arrested

Akshay, a 23-year-old Dalit man who married against his wife's family's wishes, was beaten with bricks in Khatauli on Monday and died on Tuesday. Police arrested two people on Wednesday, including her brother, and say the killing appears premeditated.

27 Oct 2026

Supreme Court to hear challenge to conversion law

The Supreme Court on Monday issued notice to the Centre and Chhattisgarh on a tribal Christian group's challenge to the state's 2026 anti-conversion law, which requires a public declaration before changing faith; neither government's response has been reported. The court tagged it with challenges to similar laws in Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan.

Supreme Court, New Delhi; Chhattisgarh · Siasat · LiveLaw
19 Oct 2026

Police detain IIT student's parents, clear protest site

Mumbai Police detained the parents of IIT Bombay student Sahil Wakode, who died by suicide on 18 September and whose family alleges caste discrimination, along with 16–22 students, and demolished a month-long campus protest site; students allege assault and were later released. Police and IIT Bombay had not commented; a case under the SC/ST Atrocities Act is registered against a professor.

IIT Bombay, Mumbai · Scroll · Indian Express · Mumbai Mirror
19 Oct 2026

Anti-mining activists jailed over Sijimali clashes

Doctor Randall Sequeira and Dalit activist Laxman Naik, who work with Adivasi communities opposing mining in Sijimali, were arrested on 16 October over April clashes in Kantamal and remanded to Rayagada jail on charges including attempted murder. Supporters say neither is named in the FIR and allege they were held overnight without lawyers; no police response was found.

Rayagada, Odisha · Countercurrents
