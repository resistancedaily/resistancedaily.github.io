Air strikes hit Myanmar clinic and school

Military air strikes on Sunday killed at least 12 people in Rakhine State, including a week-old baby at a maternity clinic in Mrauk-U, and three students at a Loikaw school, an armed group, an aid worker and monitors told Reuters; the military government did not immediately respond.

Gen Z protests spread across German cities

Youth-led rallies on Saturday over living costs, education cuts and conscription drew 5,000 in Berlin by police count and 10,000 by organisers, who claim 100,000–120,000 nationwide. Police used water cannon in Cologne, saying their measures targeted violent individuals, not peaceful participants.
