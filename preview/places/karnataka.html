29 Oct 2026

Karnataka voter roll shrinks by nearly one crore

Karnataka's final voter roll, published on 27 October after a special revision, lists about 4.55 crore electors, nearly one crore fewer than before; Bengaluru's fell from about 1.03 crore to 59.08 lakh. The Chief Electoral Officer says deletion forms covered electors found ineligible at hearings, and denies bulk filings without reasons.

Bengaluru, Karnataka · Sabrang India · NE Now

26 Oct 2026

Officials filed 61,059 voter-deletion forms without reasons

Election officials themselves filed at least 61,059 forms to delete names from Karnataka's rolls, none stating the reason the rules require, The Hindu found; most came after the 23 September deadline. Officials say the system auto-generated them when voters could not be reached. The final roll is due 27 October.

Bengaluru and across Karnataka · The Hindu
