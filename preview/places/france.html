France bans rallies, deploys 70,000 police

France is deploying about 70,000 police on Saturday and several cities have banned or rerouted protests after last weekend's rioting; 217 people have been jailed since, the justice minister says. The interior minister cited online calls for violence. Unions call the bans an abuse of authority and are challenging them in court.

20 Oct 2026

Curfews and protest bans after French riots

Authorities imposed overnight curfews in parts of Paris, Lyon, Nice and Lille on Monday and banned gatherings across Paris from 8 pm, after weekend cost-of-living and school protests of 550,000 (official count) to 800,000 (organisers) ended in looting and more than 2,330 people taken into custody. The interior minister said police had "lost control"; nearly 1,000 officers were injured on Saturday, and one protester's hand was blown off by a police crowd-control grenade.

18 Oct 2026

Hundreds of thousands march in France; 1,800 arrested

About 550,000 people marched nationwide on Saturday by the Interior Ministry's count (800,000 per the CGT union) over school conditions, living costs and budget cuts; police used tear gas and arrested more than 1,800, and officials reported 150 to over 900 police injured. Human Rights Watch says a 15-year-old lost a hand to a police stun grenade during the protests; the government blames violence on looters and says the right to protest is respected.
