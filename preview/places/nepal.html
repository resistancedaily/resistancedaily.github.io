Police detain journalists without warrants

Plainclothes police in Kathmandu took at least two journalists, Bishwamani Subedi of Nepal Press and editor Shovaraj Baral, for questioning on Friday without showing warrants or stating grounds. The Prime Minister's office said it had no prior knowledge and ordered their release; the Press Council demanded the legal basis.
