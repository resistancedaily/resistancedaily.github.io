Village raid kills 33 in Ebola-hit Congo

Attackers killed 33 people and burned a health post in Cantine, local civil society said; 180 patients, including Ebola cases, fled. Local authorities blame the ADF armed group.

2 Nov 2026

Congo police detain over 500 at Kambove mine

Police in Haut-Katanga said more than 500 people were detained in a special operation at the Kambove mining site near Likasi, Actualité.cd reported on Sunday. The provincial police chief said it targeted people illegally occupying public-service roles at the site; no charges or access to lawyers were reported.

Kambove, Haut-Katanga, DR Congo · Actualité.cd
31 Oct 2026

28.8 million face acute hunger in Congo

About 28.8 million people in DR Congo, 23 per cent of those analysed, face high acute food insecurity through December, a new IPC alert says, with North Kivu, South Kivu and Ituri hardest hit. It cites conflict, displacement, an Ebola outbreak and falling aid. No government response was reported.

Eastern DR Congo · UN News
26 Oct 2026

Nurse killed responding to Ebola alert

A nurse was killed and another health worker badly injured on Saturday in Bafwasende, Tshopo province, while responding to an Ebola alert, the WHO says. Local authorities have not commented. The outbreak, Congo's deadliest, has recorded more than 9,800 cases and 4,700 deaths since May.

Bafwasende, Tshopo province, DR Congo · AP via Newsday
19 Oct 2026

UN: 77 civilians killed in Ituri last month

UN OCHA said on Saturday that suspected ADF rebels killed at least 77 civilians in Mambasa territory in September, hitting Biakato and Bandisende hardest. The report seen carried no comment from the Congolese army.

Mambasa, Ituri, DR Congo · Anadolu via Yeni Şafak
